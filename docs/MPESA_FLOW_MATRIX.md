## Modes and evidence boundaries

- `mock` is an application simulation and never contacts Safaricom or represents money received.
- `sandbox` uses the fixed Safaricom sandbox endpoint and sandbox-aligned credentials.
- `production` uses the fixed live endpoint and requires mode-scoped live credentials and explicit callback configuration. No live payment was initiated or authorized during this audit.
- Unit/provider tests use synthetic transports. Mongo/API tests require a specifically named disposable database and do not prove Safaricom acceptance.
- Callback URL tokens are routing secrets, not Safaricom signatures. Callback amounts, phones, receipts, references and provider status are checked before settlement; conflicts stay pending for review.
- The KES 1 record is preserved and remains classified as a likely sandbox test per the user's clarification and historical source/configuration evidence. The exact historical endpoint was not durably recorded. Do not reopen or repeat it absent genuinely contradictory evidence; if such evidence appears, reconcile the official payer statement and Safaricom/merchant trace before making a payee claim.
